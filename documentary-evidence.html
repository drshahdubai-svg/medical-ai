Massachusetts General Hospital / Harvard Medical School — Dr Dinesh Patel

Letter dated 14 August 1989. Dr Dinesh Patel, Chief of the Arthroscopic Surgery Unit, wrote to confirm Dr Shah Sikkander Badusha's time with him at Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston. The letter describes close interest in arthroscopic surgical procedures involving the knee, shoulder and ankle, and opportunities to watch teaching videos and procedures. The letter documents clinical training exposure and observation; it does not establish a Harvard faculty appointment or formal Harvard degree. Original scanned letter: credentials portfolio, page 9.

Independent clinical assessment — Dr David Lhowe, Massachusetts General Hospital / Harvard Medical School

Letter dated 31 May 1994. Addressed to Mr Ahmed Saeed Al-Badi, Minister of Health, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. Dr David Lhowe wrote after visiting Mafraq Hospital as a consultant and working with Dr Shah Badusha clinically. He described Dr Badusha's work as excellent, both intellectually and technically, and expressed his professional opinion that he performed at the level of a full-fledged consultant. This is a contemporaneous independent assessment addressed to the Minister, not a certificate of appointment. Original scanned letter: credentials portfolio, page 8.

Hello Apollo — Care Beyond Boundaries

A departmental report from Cardiothoracic and Vascular Surgery at Apollo Hospitals, Dhaka records that the telephone follow-up programme began on 13 February 2011 and had served 72 patients at the time of that report. The documented departmental protocol involved telephone calls for three consecutive days after discharge, with the aim of identifying postoperative concerns and enabling timely action. Broader seven-day follow-up concepts should not be presented as the documented duration of this original departmental pilot.
